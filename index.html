Our Story

Founded in 2010, Golden Yolk began with a simple mission: to provide chefs and home cooks alike with eggs that make a real difference in the kitchen.

We partner with small family farms that prioritize animal welfare and sustainable practices. Each egg is hand-graded and packaged with care.

What sets our eggs apart? The deep golden yolks, firm whites, and rich flavor that professional kitchens demand.
